# AI tells in narrative Chinese prose

Each entry is a pattern to find and the fix. One hit can be coincidence; a cluster is the tell. This file holds what shows up most in narrative and launch posts. The general tells (grandiose framing, vague attribution, copula avoidance, emoji, Markdown leaks) are in the `zeabur-writing` skill's `ai-tells.md`.

## Sentences that sound like reflection but carry no fact

- **Panoramic verdicts**: 「整份計畫攤開來看，離完成還很遠」「回頭看這段路」「放在更大的脈絡裡」. Fix: state the concrete fact (what was unfinished, how many months in) or cut.
- **Hollow paragraph endings**: a last sentence that restates the paragraph as a lesson — 「原本……的研究，逐漸有了更廣的用途」「就這樣逐步接到……裡」「成為這次開發裡持續投入的工作」. Fix: end on the last concrete fact.
- **Announced feelings**: 「是很令人興奮的一步」「很有成就感的地方」「我們期待你……」. Fix: show what happened or what people did; keep at most one stated feeling, at the 轉 or 合.
- **Summary 合**: 「從 X、到 Y、再到 Z，原本分散的……接成了……」. Fix: one image of the reader using the result, then stop.

## Rhythm and shape

- **Tricolon**: three parallel items or clauses, again and again (「部署、查日誌、排錯」 in every paragraph). Fix: keep the one that matters; let lists of two or four happen.
- **Paired balance**: 「既能……也能……」「一部分……；另外一些……」「X 讓我們……；Y 則讓……」 as the default sentence. Fix: say the more important half.
- **Uniform sentence length**: every sentence 25–40 characters, every paragraph 4–5 sentences. Fix: break one long sentence, merge two short ones, allow a one-sentence paragraph.
- **English-style full stops**: a 句號 after every clause, so a paragraph reads as a stack of short English sentences. In Chinese, one 句號 closes one complete thought; clauses on the same topic or in a cause-effect chain join with 「，」, parallel items with 「；」, and a setup leads into its details with 「：」. Fix: merge sentences that share a subject or explain each other, and keep a 句號 where the thought actually turns. Also undo splits you made while revising; a revision that adds 句號 is usually going the wrong way.
- **Rhetorical question chains**: three questions in a row standing in for a point. Fix: keep one question the article answers, or state the problem.
- **Colon reveal**: 「起點很簡單：……」「答案是：……」. Fix: drop the setup.

## Words

- **Drift adverbs**: 「逐漸」「逐步」「慢慢」「沿著這個方向」「隨著……增加」 used to glue paragraphs. Fix: name the cause or the time.
- **Abstract nouns doing verbs' work**: 「整理成」「接到」「回到產品設計裡處理」「有了實作的機會」 when the concrete verb is known (做了, 改成, 拆成). Fix: the plain verb and its object.
- **Signature words**: 至關重要, 不可或缺, 賦能, 打造, 生態系 (metaphorical), 深入探討, 見證, 標誌著, 奠定基礎, 底層邏輯, 稳稳地/接住.
- **Negative parallelism**: 「不是 X，而是 Y」「不只是 X，更是 Y」. Fix: say Y.

## Narrative shape

- **Feature tour**: each paragraph is "then we built X" with no cost, choice or surprise. Fix: find the 轉 (see SKILL.md step 2).
- **Detail regression**: concrete specifics from the source smoothed into 「很多」「不少」「各種」. Fix: restore the number or name.
- **Invented color**: scenes, quotes or feelings the source never gave. Fix: delete and ask the author.
